---
title: Calculating zakāh after missing the zakāh date
category: zakah
topic: Calculating Zakāh
date: '2026-10-24'
question: A person's annual zakāh date is the 15th of Ramaḍān, but they forgot to pay on that date. Today is the 15th of Rabīʿ al-Thānī. How should they calculate the zakāh they owe, and which assets do they need to pay it on?
draft: false
short_answer: The person should calculate their annual net assets on the 15th of Ramaḍān, and pay the zakāh owed on that basis. They would be sinful for the delay if it was done on purpose.
explanation: []
hikmah: |-
  First, a person should think of zakāh not as a charity, but as a responsibility given to them by the Divine, subḥānahu wa taʿālā. Its inherent goal is to show the individual that the main purpose behind owning wealth is realising the actual Owner and Master Sustainer of the universe, subḥānahu wa taʿālā, understanding how one's realms of responsibility fall in regard to Him, subḥānahu wa taʿālā, and how, as an individual, one needs to maintain that relationship. It creates a manifest understanding within oneself that the ultimate Owner is Allah subḥānahu wa taʿālā, the ultimate Sustainer is Allah subḥānahu wa taʿālā, and wealth is only given by Allah subḥānahu wa taʿālā.

  Secondly, as for why a person should hasten and try their best to relieve themselves of this responsibility as soon as possible if they failed to fulfil it on time: just as a person has assets, debts and liabilities that are incurred in this world, a person similarly has assets and liabilities that are incurred in the Ākhirah. A person can think of these as their good deeds, their sins, and their missed opportunities or missed aʿmāl. In that regard, if a person were to imagine that on the Day of Judgment, due to laziness or inattentiveness, they had been unable to complete one of those prescribed aʿmāl, it would be a big loss for them. In order to prevent that loss, it would be best practice to complete the action as soon as one is able to.
