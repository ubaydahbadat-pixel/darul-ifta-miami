biography: |-
  Abū ʿAlī al-Ḥasan ibn Ziyād al-Luʾluʾī was of Kufan origin and settled in Baghdad. He was one of the most gifted in raʾy, a lover and follower of the Sunnah, learned in the narrations from Abū Ḥanīfah, and foremost in questioning and deriving rulings. Yaḥyā ibn Ādam said of him: "I have not seen anyone more learned in fiqh than al-Ḥasan ibn Ziyād."

  He studied with Abū Ḥanīfah for a time, and after his death with Zufar and Abū Yūsuf. He was appointed qāḍī but it did not suit him and he asked to be relieved, devoting himself instead to fatwā and teaching. He died in 204 AH, in his eighties, leaving works in fiqh and ḥadīth.
