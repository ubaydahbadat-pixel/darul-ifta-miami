  ʿAlī ibn al-Ḥasan ibn Muḥammad Abū al-Ḥasan al-Balkhī, known as al-Burhān, was an ascetic shaykh and imām of great standing, one of those who spread knowledge in the lands of Islam. He was the first to teach at several madrasahs when they were founded, and taught in Bukhārā and Aleppo. He died in al-Shām in 548 AH.
