biography: |-
  Abū Shibl ʿAlqamah ibn Qays ibn ʿAbdullāh al-Nakhaʿī al-Kūfī was a Tābiʿī, born in the lifetime of Rasūlullāh ﷺ. He was the companion of ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd and took his knowledge from him, and of all people he most resembled him in guidance and bearing.

  Of Ibn Masʿūd's companions he had the greatest share of his knowledge, fiqh and ḥadīth. He stayed with him until he graduated under him, and after him took up fatwā and leadership. He died between 61 and 73 AH.
