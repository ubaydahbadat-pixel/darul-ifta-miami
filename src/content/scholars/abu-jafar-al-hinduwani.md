short_name: Abū Jaʿfar al-Hinduwānī
biography: |-
  Abū Jaʿfar Muḥammad ibn ʿAbdullāh ibn Muḥammad ibn ʿUmar al-Hinduwānī al-Balkhī was one of the great Ḥanafī jurists of his time. For his fiqh he was called "Abū Ḥanīfah al-Ṣaghīr". A faqīh of great standing and remarkable intelligence, he used to say of himself: "If all the sayings of Abū Ḥanīfah, the dictations of Abū Yūsuf, and the nawādir and ziyādāt of Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan were burned, I could write them out from memory without putting a letter before or after its place."

  He took from al-Aʿmash, al-Iskāf and al-Ṣaffār, and Abū al-Layth al-Samarqandī and other scholars of the madhhab studied with him. He died in 362 AH.
