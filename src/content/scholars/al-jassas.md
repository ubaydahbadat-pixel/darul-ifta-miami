biography: |-
  Abū Bakr Aḥmad ibn ʿAlī al-Rāzī al-Jaṣṣāṣ was a faqīh, uṣūlī and mufassir, and the leadership of the Ḥanafīs passed to him in his time. He studied fiqh with Abū al-Ḥasan al-Karkhī and lived in Baghdad, where he died in 370 AH.

  His works include Aḥkām al-Qurʾān, his Uṣūl, his abridgement of al-Ṭaḥāwī's Ikhtilāf al-ʿUlamāʾ, and commentaries on al-Jāmiʿ al-Kabīr, al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr, Mukhtaṣar al-Ṭaḥāwī and Mukhtaṣar al-Karkhī.
