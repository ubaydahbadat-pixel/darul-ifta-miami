biography: |-
  Abū Bakr Muḥammad ibn Aḥmad ibn Abī Sahl al-Sarakhsī, Shams al-Aʾimmah, was one of the great imāms: Sarakhsī by origin, Bukhārī by homeland, Farghānī in his teaching, Ḥanafī in knowledge, and buried in Balkh.

  His works include his Uṣūl, al-Mabsūṭ, al-Muḥīṭ, and commentaries on al-Jāmiʿ al-Kabīr, al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr, al-Siyar al-Kabīr, Ziyādāt al-Ziyādāt and Mukhtaṣar al-Ṭaḥāwī. Al-Naqīb gives his death as around 490 AH; Mashāyikh Balkh gives 481 AH.
