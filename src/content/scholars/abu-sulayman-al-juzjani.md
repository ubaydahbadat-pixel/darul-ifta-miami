short_name: Abū Sulaymān al-Jūzjānī
biography: |-
  Abū Sulaymān Mūsā ibn Sulaymān al-Jūzjānī al-Balkhī heard from ʿAbdullāh ibn al-Mubārak, and from Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan and Abū Yūsuf among the companions of Abū Ḥanīfah. He narrated their books and dictations (amālī), and of those who did so with al-Muʿallā ibn Manṣūr he was the older and the better known.

  A perceptive faqīh in raʾy, he made his own copies of Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan's books and narrated them from him, with differences from the narration of Abū Ḥafṣ al-Bukhārī. Through him the books of Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan spread in Khurāsān, where he lived and taught ḥadīth. He died after 200 AH.
biography_source: al-Naqīb, al-Madhhab al-Ḥanafī, p. 115; al-Mudarris, Mashāyikh Balkh min al-Ḥanafiyyah, p. 85, no. 23 (translated)
