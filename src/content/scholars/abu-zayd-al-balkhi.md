biography: |-
  Aḥmad ibn Sahl Abū Zayd al-Balkhī was one of the outstanding great scholars of Islam, combining the Sharīʿah with philosophy, literature and the arts. He was born in one of the villages of Balkh and travelled widely before returning. The ruler of Balkh offered him the vizierate and he refused; the post of secretary was then put to him and he accepted it, though it vexed him until he died in Balkh.

  He preceded the scholars of Islam in drawing maps of the earth, in his book Ṣuwar al-Aqālīm al-Islāmiyyah. Ibn al-Nadīm lists his works, among them al-Siyāsah al-Kabīr, al-Siyāsah al-Ṣaghīr, Mā Yaṣiḥḥu min Aḥkām al-Nujūm, a book on chess, Faḍāʾil Balkh and Naẓm al-Qurʾān.
