biography: |-
  Muḥammad ibn Shujāʿ al-Thaljī was the faqīh of Iraq in his time and one of the best-known students of al-Ḥasan ibn Ziyād; it was said that he "opened up the fiqh of Abū Ḥanīfah, argued for it, brought out its reasons and strengthened it with ḥadīth".

  He was devoted to worship, night prayer and recitation, and was known for his knowledge of ḥadīth, though the muḥaddithūn considered him weak, and he had leanings towards the Muʿtazilah. His works include al-Muḍārabah, al-Manāsik and al-Nawādir. He died in 266 AH.
