  ʿAbd al-Rashīd ibn Abī Ḥanīfah al-Nuʿmān Abū al-Fatḥ al-Walwālijī al-Badakhshī was an imām of distinction and vast learning, Ḥanafī in madhhab. He came to Balkh and studied fiqh there, with Abū Bakr al-Qadhdhār among others, and is the author of al-Fatāwā al-Walwālijiyyah. He was born in Walwālij and died there in 540 AH.
