biography: |-
  Abū Ḥanīfah al-Nuʿmān ibn Thābit al-Kūfī was born in Kufa in 80 AH, lived most of his life there, and died in Baghdad in 150 AH at the age of seventy.

  He began as a merchant, until al-Shaʿbī noticed his intelligence and urged him to study and to sit with the scholars. In 102 AH, at the age of twenty-two, he turned to fiqh and joined the circle of Ḥammād ibn Abī Sulaymān, the faqīh of Kufa, staying with him for eighteen years until Ḥammād's death.

  When Ḥammād died in 120 AH, his companions chose Abū Ḥanīfah, then forty, to lead the circle. He taught and gave fatwā for some thirty years, and a great number of students trained under him, many of whom became imāms in their own right.
