biography: |-
  Abū Ismāʿīl Ḥammād ibn Abī Sulaymān Muslim al-Kūfī, the faqīh of Iraq, was known for his intelligence and generosity, and was a man of wealth and dignity. He narrated from Anas ibn Mālik, Saʿīd ibn al-Musayyib, al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī and others.

  He studied fiqh with Ibrāhīm al-Nakhaʿī, the faqīh of Kufa in his time, and was the most distinguished of his companions. After al-Nakhaʿī's death he became the muftī of Kufa. Abū Ḥanīfah took most of his knowledge from him and stayed with him for eighteen years. He died in 120 AH.
