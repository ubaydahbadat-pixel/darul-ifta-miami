biography: |-
  ʿĀfiyah ibn Yazīd ibn Qays al-Awdī al-Kūfī was one of the leading imāms and a companion of Imām Abū Ḥanīfah. When ʿĀfiyah was absent from a discussion, Abū Ḥanīfah would tell his companions not to settle the question until ʿĀfiyah was present. Al-Mahdī appointed him qāḍī of the eastern side of Baghdad. He died after 160 AH.
