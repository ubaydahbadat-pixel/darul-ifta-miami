biography: |-
  Abū al-Layth Naṣr ibn Muḥammad al-Samarqandī al-Balkhī, called Imām al-Hudā, was the well-known faqīh, author of useful positions and famous works. He studied and taught in Balkh, and studied with Abū Jaʿfar al-Hinduwānī. He gathered the fatāwā of the people of Balkh in his al-Nawāzil, and was known simply as "al-Faqīh". He dictated to his students the books of Muḥammad and Abū Yūsuf together with his own commentaries on them.

  His works also include a Tafsīr, a commentary on al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr, Khizānat al-Fiqh, ʿUyūn al-Masāʾil and Mukhtalif al-Riwāyah. Al-Naqīb gives his death as between 373 and 393 AH; Mashāyikh Balkh says he died in Balkh in 375 AH, according to the soundest view.
