biography: |-
  Abū ʿAbdullāh Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan ibn Farqad al-Shaybānī was born in Wāsiṭ in the first half of the 130s AH and raised in Kufa. He lived in Baghdad and taught ḥadīth there, and died in al-Rayy in 189 AH.

  One of the most brilliant of people, a leading imām in fiqh and skilled in Arabic, grammar and arithmetic, he studied with Abū Ḥanīfah in the last part of his life, then with Abū Yūsuf, until he mastered fiqh; he also heard from Imām Mālik and others. Imām al-Shāfiʿī studied with him and stayed with him, and said: "I have never sat with anyone more learned in fiqh."

  He was appointed qāḍī by al-Rashīd, and recorded the Ḥanafī fiqh in books, among them al-Mabsūṭ (also called al-Aṣl), al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr, al-Jāmiʿ al-Kabīr, al-Ḥujjah ʿalā Ahl al-Madīnah, al-Ziyādāt, Ziyādāt al-Ziyādāt and al-Siyar.
