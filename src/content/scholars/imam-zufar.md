biography: |-
  Abū al-Hudhayl Zufar ibn al-Hudhayl ibn Qays al-ʿAnbarī, the eldest of Abū Ḥanīfah's four great companions, was born in 110 AH. He was one of the oceans of fiqh and among the most intelligent of his time: a man of intellect, religion, understanding and scrupulousness, who combined knowledge, worship, fiqh and ḥadīth.

  He studied fiqh with Abū Ḥanīfah and sat with him for a long time, and Abū Ḥanīfah honoured him greatly. After Abū Ḥanīfah's death, Zufar succeeded him in his teaching circle. He was appointed qāḍī of Basra and died there in 158 AH, eight years after his teacher.
