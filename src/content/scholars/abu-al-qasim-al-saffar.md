biography: |-
  Aḥmad ibn Ḥām ibn ʿIṣmah (or ʿAḍd) Abū al-Qāsim al-Ṣaffār al-Balkhī was one of the great figures and jurists of Balkh. He reached such a level in fiqh and confidence in his own reasoning that he said: "I differed from Abū Ḥanīfah in a thousand questions, giving fatwā by my own preference and ijtihād, and the fatwā today is on my view in these thousand questions."

  He lived from the earnings of his own hands, and people competed to study with him. He took from Nuṣayr ibn Yaḥyā, and Abū Jaʿfar al-Hinduwānī took from him. He died in 326 or 336 AH.
