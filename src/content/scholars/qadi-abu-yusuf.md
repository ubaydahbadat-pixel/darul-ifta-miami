biography: |-
  Abū Yūsuf Yaʿqūb ibn Ibrāhīm ibn Ḥabīb al-Anṣārī was born in 113 AH. A faqīh and scholar who knew tafsīr, the maghāzī and the history of the Arabs, he was held in high regard by Hārūn al-Rashīd, and in his time was given authority over judgeships from the east to the west.

  He studied first with Ibn Abī Laylā, then went to Abū Ḥanīfah, studied fiqh with him and stayed with him until his death; he said that he accompanied him for seventeen years. Imāms such as Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan and Aḥmad ibn Ḥanbal took from him.

  He died in Baghdad in 182 AH, having served as qāḍī there under three ʿAbbāsid caliphs: al-Mahdī, al-Hādī and al-Rashīd. Among his works are Kitāb al-Āthār, Ikhtilāf Abī Ḥanīfah wa Ibn Abī Laylā, al-Kharāj and al-Radd ʿalā Siyar al-Awzāʿī.
