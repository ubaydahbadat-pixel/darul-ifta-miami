## 1. Answers that don't (fully) answer the question

- [ ] **Bleeding during pregnancy** — live with the question only: no short answer, Explanation, ḥikmah or references.
- [ ] **Wudhu on cast** — the question asks about two stages (the first cast must stay dry; the second bandage can take some water). The short answer covers only the first ("avoid wiping over the cast until the doctor changes the bandage") and says nothing about the second bandage, or what to do with the finger meanwhile.
- [ ] **Calculating zakāh** — the question asks (a) how to calculate and (b) *which assets* to pay on. The short answer covers (a) only.
- [ ] **Buying the uḍḥiyah animal early** — the question's first part asks whether keeping the animal from early on "brings more reward. Is that correct?" The short answer says it is "more beneficial to have an animal with which a person has a deep emotional attachment" but does not say yes or no to the reward claim. (Ages and care are answered.)
- [ ] **Iḥrām via Qatar** — the question asks when to *put on the iḥrām* and *make the niyyah*. The short answer gives the place for the intention only; putting on the iḥrām garments is not addressed separately.
- [ ] **Uḍḥiyah with ḥarām wealth** — answers where the person's ḥalāl wealth reaches the niṣāb; does not say what applies if their ḥalāl wealth is below the niṣāb (the question says the ḥarām money is "most of my wealth").
- [ ] **Adding rakats after the final sitting** — the congregation is not addressed (see fatwa-01 review, C14).
- Answers that match their questions: Missed Ramaḍān fasts · Qaṣr from West Palm Beach · Alcohol-based perfume · Botox injections.
