# Review — Fatwa 1: Adding rakats after the final sitting

Status: **Open — for the Mufti's review (Saturday, 24 October 2026).** Not published on the site.
Audit date: 22 October 2026. Each item describes where the page differs from, or goes beyond, the reference texts. Tick items off as they are resolved.

## A. Statements the reference texts don't support, or state differently

- [ ] **A1. The question and the "full sitting".** Node 2 and its sources (al-Qudūrī, *al-Muḥīṭ*, *ʿUmdat al-Fatāwā*) all depend on the Imām having sat in the fourth rakʿah for the length of the tashahhud. The question says he believed he was in the second rakʿah but does not state that he sat for that length.
- [ ] **A2. Node 1 — "the ḥadīth of Thawbān and ʿUmar".** The *Sharḥ Mukhtaṣar al-Karkhī* and *al-Muḥīṭ* give the basis as the Prophet ﷺ forgetting and prostrating, plus the ḥadīth of Thawbān. ʿUmar is not named there; his narration appears only later in *al-Muḥīṭ*, about the completed prayer (node 2).
- [ ] **A3. Node 1 — al-Karkhī's view.** The *Sharḥ* reports it with «حُكي» and immediately records that others of our companions held sajdat al-sahw to be sunnah. The Explanation gives only the wājib view.
- [ ] **A4. Node 1 — the six causes.** *al-Muḥīṭ* attributes them to "most of the mashāyikh" («وأكثرهم على أنه يجب بستة أشياء»), not to Ibn Māzah himself.
- [ ] **A5. Node 2 — "cycles of two".** *al-Muḥīṭ* makes two separate points: less than a full rakʿah has no ruling of prayer («ما دون الركعة ليس له حكم الصلاة»), so he returns to the sitting; and a completed extra rakʿah is nafl, and nafl is prayed in pairs («فإن النفل شرع شفعاً لا وتراً»). The Explanation says rakʿahs in general are in cycles of two.
- [ ] **A6. Node 2 — the reason for the sajdah.** *al-Muḥīṭ*: because he left the wording of the salām («لأنه ترك لفظة السلام»), which is wājib; and this is the istiḥsān answer, while by qiyās no sajdah is due. The Explanation says he *delayed* the salām and does not mention istiḥsān/qiyās.
- [ ] **A7. Node 2 — the ʿAṣr objection.** Presented as a possible objection. *al-Muḥīṭ* records it as an actual view of some mashāyikh («قال بعضهم: يقطع، ولا يضيف إلى الخامسة ركعة أخرى»), which Imām Muḥammad indicated in *Ziyādāt al-Ziyādāt*, before giving the other view via al-Ḥasan and Hishām.
- [ ] **A8. Node 2 — the āyah (47:33) as "the primary reason".** *al-Muḥīṭ*'s stated reason is that what is makrūh is *beginning* a voluntary prayer, not becoming engaged in one through forgetfulness. The āyah is not in any of the references.
- [ ] **A9. Node 3 — "a narration".** Ibn ʿĀbidīn introduces the Durar/Nihāyah view with «وقيل» (and it is said). Node 3 calls it "a narration" without that qualifier.
- [ ] **A10. Short Answer — "invalid".** No reference uses that word. Dār al-ʿUlūm Zakariyyā: after leaving the masjid, repetition is necessary («اعادہ ضروری ہے»). al-Durr: speaking ends the taḥrīmah («لبطلان التحريمة»). Node 1 quotes al-Karkhī that sajdat al-sahw is "not a condition for the validity of the prayer".

## B. Wording and consistency

- [ ] **B11. "Obligatory" for wājib in node 1** (three places: sajdat al-sahw; al-Karkhī's view; "altering an obligatory action"). Node 2 now uses "wājib (necessary)".
- [ ] **B12. Node 1 — al-Qudūrī's list.** "Sunnah (necessary) action" renders «فعلا مسنونا». Node 1 labels Fātiḥah, Qunūt, Tashahhud and the ʿĪd takbīrs as "wājib"; the *Mukhtaṣar* does not call them that in this passage, and also lists the imām's loud/silent recitation, which node 1 leaves out.
- [ ] **B13. Node 3.** Leaves out the ṣulbiyyah prostration, which al-Durr lists with sahw and tilāwah. Names "*al-ʿInāyah* and *Fatḥ al-Qadīr*" as two works; the citation is one source — *Sharḥ al-ʿInāyah* printed in the margin of *Fatḥ al-Qadīr*.

## C. Coverage of the question

- [ ] **C14. The congregation.** The question involves a congregation; the answer does not say what applies to those behind the Imām. Whether it should is the Mufti's decision.
